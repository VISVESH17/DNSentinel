How a decision is made

1. The domain is normalized (TLD, subdomain depth, entropy).
2. It's checked against synced threat-intelligence indicators (+60 max).
3. A local ML model scores DGA/algorithmic-domain likelihood (+20 max).
4. Lexical entropy anomalies and behavioural tunnelling signals add further weight (+10 / +15).
5. The combined 0–100 score maps to ALLOW / MONITOR / ALERT / BLOCK.
